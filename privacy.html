Privacy Policy

Squeeze It In! · Effective date: 2026-10-07

In short

Squeeze It In! (行李箱大作战 / 行李箱大作戰) is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Xiupeng Wu (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The game doesn’t collect personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it doesn’t connect to any server of ours: we don’t have one. Your game progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves these things on your device: which trips you’ve packed, your stars and best times, your Daily Pack results and streak, your best Whirlwind Tour, your best time on each timed challenge, which trophies you’ve already seen, which tutorial steps and rule cards you’ve seen, the last trip you opened, the trip you were in the middle of when you left the game, and whether music, sound effects and haptics are on.

The game also keeps a short log of recent technical errors on your device (the error message, where in the game it happened, the game’s language, and the app and iOS versions). You can clear it in the game’s Settings under Feedback & Support.

It also notes a few times: when the game last asked you to rate it and for which version, and whether and when it showed the Game Center sign-in prompt. These only decide how often you’re asked.

All of this is stored inside the app on your device. We can’t see it, and the game never sends it to us on its own. The only exception is the technical information included in a feedback email that you choose to send (see “Emails you send us” below).

If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is turned on, iOS includes this data in those backups. Backups are handled by Apple or by your computer, not by us.

Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

Saving cards to Photos

The first time you save a Trip Card or Trophy Card, iOS asks whether the game may add photos. The game asks only for “Add Photos Only” access: it can add the card image to your library, but it can’t see or read your photos.

You can change this at any time in the Settings app, in this app’s Photos setting.

Sharing cards

When you tap a Share button, the iOS share sheet opens with the card image and a short line of text. Nothing is shared until you choose where to send it. Whatever you share is then handled by the app or service you choose, under its own privacy policy.

Game Center

If you’re signed in to Game Center, the game sends Apple’s Game Center the trophies you’ve earned (as achievements) and your leaderboard scores: the most places you’ve reached in one Whirlwind Tour and your best time on each of the three timed challenges. Game Center is provided by Apple and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

We don’t run any server, so we don’t receive your Game Center data, your Apple Account email address or your real name. You can sign out of Game Center or change its settings in the Settings app.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and everything you put in the email. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems.

Where the game offers a Feedback option, it opens a draft email in your mail app. The draft may include a short technical summary in the body of the email, such as the app version, iOS version, device model and recent error messages from the game. It is all in the body, so you can read, change or delete it before sending. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

We keep support emails only as long as we need them to handle your request, and no longer than 12 months after it’s resolved. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Information Apple may share with us

If you’ve chosen in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics for this app. Apple decides what these reports contain. We use them only to find and fix problems.

App Store Connect also shows us totals such as the number of downloads. These totals don’t identify you.

No tracking, no ads, no third parties

We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we don’t show ads. The game contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. We don’t sell or share personal data.

Children

The game is meant for everyone. It doesn’t collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Your game data is on your own device, so you’re in control of it: use Reset Progress in the game’s Settings to erase your progress, or delete the app to delete all of its data, and use the Settings app to change Photos or Game Center access.

We don’t hold any of your game data, so there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete. For emails you’ve sent us, write to wuxiupengftd@gmail.com to ask for a copy or ask us to delete them.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under data protection law. Contact us at the same address to use them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the new version here and update the effective date. If a future version of the game starts collecting any data, we’ll update this policy and the App Store privacy information before that version is released.

简要说明

「行李箱大作战：最后一件，塞进去！」（英文名 Squeeze It In!）是 吴秀鹏（下称「我们」）开发的 iPhone 解谜游戏。本政策说明你玩这个游戏时，你的信息会怎样处理。

这个游戏不收集个人数据。它没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析，也没有接入任何第三方 SDK；它不连接我们的服务器，我们也没有服务器。你的游戏进度只保存在你的设备上。

保存在你设备上的内容

为了记住进度，游戏会在你的设备上保存这些内容：收好了哪些旅行、星星和最快用时、今日一箱的成绩和连续天数、特种兵旅行的最好成绩、限时挑战的最快用时、已经看过的奖杯、看过的新手引导和规则说明、最近打开的关卡、离开游戏时没收完的那一局，以及音乐、音效和触感的开关。

游戏还会在设备上留一份最近的技术错误记录（错误信息、出错的位置、游戏语言、App 和 iOS 的版本），可以在游戏设置的「反馈与支持」里清空。

另外还记着几个时间点：上次请求你给 App 评分的时间和版本、Game Center 登录提示是否出现过、什么时候出现的。它们只用来控制多久问你一次。

以上内容都保存在设备上的 App 里，我们看不到，游戏也不会自动发给我们。唯一的例外是你自己发反馈邮件时，正文里附上的那段技术信息（见下文「你发给我们的邮件」）。

如果你开着 iCloud 云备份或电脑备份，iOS 会把这些数据一起放进备份。备份由 Apple 或你的电脑管理，不经过我们。

删除 App 会把这些数据从设备上一起删除。

保存卡片到相册

第一次保存纪念卡或奖杯卡时，iOS 会询问是否允许游戏添加照片。游戏只申请「仅添加照片」权限：可以把卡片图片存进相册，但看不到、也读不到你的照片。

你随时可以在「设置」App 里找到本 App，修改「照片」权限。

分享卡片

点分享按钮时，会打开 iOS 的系统分享面板，里面是卡片图片和一句文字。在你选好分享到哪里之前，什么都不会发出去。分享出去的内容，由你选择的 App 或服务按它自己的隐私政策处理。

Game Center

如果你登录了 Game Center，游戏会把这些内容发送给 Apple 的 Game Center：已经拿到的奖杯（作为成就），以及排行榜成绩——特种兵旅行一趟最多去过几个地方、三条限时挑战各自的最快用时。Game Center 由 Apple 提供，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

我们没有服务器，收不到你的 Game Center 数据，也拿不到你的 Apple 账户邮箱或真实姓名。你可以在「设置」App 里退出 Game Center 或修改它的设置。

你发给我们的邮件

如果你给我们发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件里的全部内容，只用来回复你和修复问题。

如果游戏里提供「反馈」入口，点它会在你的邮件 App 里生成一封草稿。草稿正文里可能附有一段技术信息，例如 App 版本、iOS 版本、设备型号和游戏最近的报错信息。这些都写在正文里，你可以在发送前查看、修改或删掉。只有你点了「发送」，邮件才会发出。

支持邮件我们只保留到处理完你的问题为止，最长不超过问题解决后 12 个月。你也可以要求我们提前删除。

Apple 可能提供给我们的信息

如果你在 iPhone 设置里选择了与 App 开发者共享分析数据，Apple 可能会把本 App 的崩溃报告和使用统计提供给我们。报告里有什么由 Apple 决定，我们只用来查找和修复问题。

App Store Connect 还会向我们显示下载量之类的汇总数字。这些汇总数字识别不到你个人。

不追踪、无广告、无第三方

我们不会跨其他公司的 App 或网站追踪你，也不展示广告。游戏里没有任何统计分析、广告或崩溃收集类的第三方 SDK。我们不出售、不共享个人数据。

儿童

这个游戏适合所有人。它不向任何人收集个人数据，包括儿童。

你的选择

游戏数据在你自己的设备上，由你掌控：在游戏的设置里点「重置进度」可以清除进度，删除 App 会删除它的全部数据；相册和 Game Center 的权限可以在「设置」App 里修改。

我们手里没有你的游戏数据，所以没有可以查询、导出或删除的内容。对于你发给我们的邮件，可以写信到 wuxiupengftd@gmail.com 索取副本或要求删除。

政策变更
