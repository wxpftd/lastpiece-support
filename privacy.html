Privacy Policy

Packing Diary · Effective date: 2026-10-07

In short

Packing Diary (装箱日记 / 裝箱日記) is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad made by Xiupeng Wu (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The game doesn’t collect personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it doesn’t connect to any server of ours: we don’t have one. Your game progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The app saves trip progress, unfinished puzzles, stars, photos from completed trips, magnet arrangements, challenge records and preferences on this device. It also keeps local records for Game Center reporting and rating prompts. If enabled, device backups may include this data. Reset All Progress clears your journey in the game; deleting the app removes its local data.

Saving cards to Photos

If you choose to save a travel photo from the system share sheet, iOS handles that action. The game does not read your photo library.

Sharing cards

When you tap a Share button, the iOS share sheet opens with the card image and a short line of text. Nothing is shared until you choose where to send it. Whatever you share is then handled by the app or service you choose, under its own privacy policy.

Game Center

When you use Game Center, the game reports earned achievements and your best results in Timed Rush and Endless Journey to Apple. Apple operates Game Center. We do not receive your Apple Account email or payment details. You can manage Game Center in your device settings.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and everything you put in the email. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems.

Where the game offers a Feedback option, it opens a draft email in your mail app. The draft may include a short technical summary in the body of the email, such as the app version, iOS version, device model and recent error messages from the game. It is all in the body, so you can read, change or delete it before sending. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

We keep support emails only as long as we need them to handle your request, and no longer than 12 months after it’s resolved. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

In-app purchases

The Full Version is sold as a one-time In-App Purchase through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details, name or Apple Account information.

To unlock the Full Version, the game checks your purchase with Apple on your device each time it starts. Nothing about your purchase is sent to us. App Store Connect shows us sales totals, which don’t identify you.

Information Apple may share with us

If you’ve chosen in your iPhone or iPad settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics for this app. Apple decides what these reports contain. We use them only to find and fix problems.

App Store Connect also shows us totals such as the number of downloads. These totals don’t identify you.

No tracking, no ads, no third parties

We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we don’t show ads. The game contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. We don’t sell or share personal data.

Children

The game is meant for everyone. It doesn’t collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Your game data is on your own device, so you’re in control of it: use Reset Progress in the game’s Settings to erase your progress, or delete the app to delete all of its data, and use the Settings app to change Photos or Game Center access.

We don’t hold any of your game data, so there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete. For emails you’ve sent us, write to wuxiupengftd@gmail.com to ask for a copy or ask us to delete them.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under data protection law. Contact us at the same address to use them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the new version here and update the effective date. If a future version of the game starts collecting any data, we’ll update this policy and the App Store privacy information before that version is released.

Contact

Xiupeng Wu

Email: wuxiupengftd@gmail.com

進行状況、途中のパズル、星、旅の写真、マグネットの配置、チャレンジ記録、各種設定を端末に保存します。Game Center への報告や評価の案内に使う記録も端末に保存します。端末のバックアップが有効な場合、これらがバックアップされることがあります。旅の進行状況はゲームの設定からリセットでき、アプリを削除すると端末内のアプリデータが消去されます。

共有ボタンをタップすると、カード画像と短いテキストがセットされた iOS の共有シートが開きます。送信先を自分で選ぶまで、外部へは何も送信されません。共有した内容は、選択したアプリやサービスそれぞれのプライバシーポリシーに従って処理されます。

요약

패킹 다이어리 (Packing Diary)는 吴秀鹏(이하 ‘당사’)가 제작한 iPhone 및 iPad용 퍼즐 게임입니다. 본 방침은 게임을 플레이할 때 이용자의 정보가 어떻게 처리되는지 설명합니다.

이 게임은 개인정보를 수집하지 않습니다. 계정, 광고, 분석 도구, 제3자 SDK가 없으며, 당사가 운영하는 서버도 없으므로 어떠한 자체 서버에도 연결하지 않습니다. 게임 진행 기록은 오직 이용자의 기기에만 저장됩니다.

기기에만 저장되는 정보

게임 진행 상황, 진행 중인 퍼즐, 별, 여행 사진, 자석 배치, 도전 기록과 설정을 이 기기에 저장합니다. Game Center 보고 및 평가 안내를 위한 기록도 기기에 저장합니다. 기기 백업을 켜면 이 데이터가 백업에 포함될 수 있습니다. 설정에서 여정을 초기화할 수 있고, 앱을 삭제하면 기기 내 앱 데이터가 지워집니다.

‘사진’ 앱에 카드 저장하기

시스템 공유 화면에서 여행 사진 저장을 선택하면 iOS가 처리합니다. 게임은 사진 보관함을 읽지 않습니다.

카드 공유하기

공유 버튼을 탭하면 카드 이미지와 짧은 문구가 담긴 iOS 공유 시트가 열립니다. 이용자가 전송할 대상을 직접 선택하기 전까지는 아무것도 공유되지 않습니다. 공유 이후의 처리는 선택한 앱이나 서비스의 개인정보 처리방침을 따릅니다.

Game Center

Game Center를 이용하면 달성한 업적과 시간 도전 및 무한 여정의 최고 기록을 Apple에 전송합니다. Game Center는 Apple이 운영합니다. 개발자는 Apple 계정 이메일 주소나 결제 정보를 받지 않습니다. 기기 설정에서 Game Center를 관리할 수 있습니다.

이용자가 보내는 이메일
